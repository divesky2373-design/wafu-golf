# 和风球场

三座十八洞球场的高尔夫网页游戏。东京（把城市当球场）：穿雷门、坐屋形船过河、楼顶打楼顶、让山手线把球捎到果岭、借银座的玻璃幕墙反弹、坐电梯上涩谷的楼顶、歌舞伎町的弹珠台、摩天轮、台场的烟火；和风球场：富士山、千本鸟居、枯山水、高千穗峡、金阁倒映的镜池；桂林山水（水墨画风）：象鼻山、月亮山、暗河岩洞、会把球弹起来的铜鼓、江心的沙洲、水田、龙脊梯田。用一笔手势挥杆，靠杆面和甩杆的方向把球打弯，压低的球还能在水面上打水漂；上了果岭读坡推杆。

A free browser golf game with three 18-hole courses. Tokyo, where the city is the course — drive through the Thunder Gate, ride a pleasure boat across the Sumida, play roof to roof, let the loop-line train carry your ball to the green, bank it off the glass in Ginza, take the lift to a rooftop in Shibuya, rattle through pachinko bumpers in Kabukicho, ride the big wheel, catch a firework over Odaiba. Japan — Mount Fuji, a tunnel of torii, a raked dry garden, Takachiho Gorge, the Golden Pavilion on its mirror pond. Guilin, in ink wash — skip a ball under Elephant Trunk Hill, fly one through Moon Hill, skim it into a river cave, bounce it off a bronze drum, hop from sandbar to sandbar. Swing with one brush-stroke gesture, shape every shot, skip low ones across the water, read the greens. Chinese and English, mouse or touch.

在线玩 / Play: https://divesky2373-design.github.io/wafu-golf/

- 鼠标：光标移到球上（不用按），向下拉，再向上甩过球
- 触屏：手指按在球上，向下拉，再向上甩
- 键盘：← → 杆面，↑ ↓ 弹道，Q E 瞄准，Z X 换杆，按住 V 俯瞰

画面现场生成，声音现场合成，没有外部素材。`index.html` 是打包好的单个文件，下载后不联网也能打开。
画面引擎是 [three.js](https://threejs.org/)（MIT 许可，版权声明在 `index.html` 末尾）。
